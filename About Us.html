About VECKO

VECKO brings your videos and music together in one clean, fast app. Add files or whole folders, browse your library, and play almost anything with controls that stay out of your way. When you need a different format, the built-in converter does it right on your Mac.

Play everythingMP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM, MP3, FLAC, AAC and many more formats.
OrganizeLibrary, favorites, recently played, playlists and a queue you can reorder.
ConvertTurn videos into other formats, extract audio, or save a frame or GIF.
Control playbackSpeed from 0.25× to 2×, custom skip time, and Picture-in-Picture.
SubtitlesAdd SRT, VTT or ASS files, then resize and sync them.
SoundA 5-band equalizer with presets and audio visualizers.

Private by design

Your media stays on your Mac. VECKO has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it never uploads your files. Read the full Privacy Policy.

VECKO Premium

Core playback is free. Premium unlocks the converter, equalizer and visualizers, speed and skip controls, subtitles, Picture-in-Picture, and playlists and queue. It is available as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Details are in the Terms of Use.
