Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

The short version. VECKO plays, organizes and converts the videos and music on your Mac. Your media never leaves your device. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not collect or sell your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how VECKO (the "App"), provided by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not upload, copy, scan or view your videos, music, subtitles, playlists or file names. All playback, library management and conversion happens on your device.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software in the App, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

2. Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following only on your Mac, so it can work the way you set it up:

  • Settings: theme, language, skip interval, subtitle size, sidebar state, library layout and sort order.
  • Library data: references to the files you add, playlists, favorites, recently played items and playback positions.
  • Status flags: whether you finished the introduction, whether you have Premium, and whether you have already rated the App.

This information is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by deleting the App.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is offered as in-app purchases (weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase). Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your Apple ID, payment card or billing details. The App receives only confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active, so it can unlock Premium features. Apple's handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Internet use

VECKO works offline. It connects to the internet only to:

  • check your Premium status with the App Store (when you are online);
  • open pages such as this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, the EULA and About Us inside the App, which are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address when the page loads, under its own privacy policy;
  • open links you choose, such as the App Store, your mail app or your browser.

5. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it.

6. Third-party services

The only third parties involved are Apple (App Store, in-app purchases) and the page host described above. Links to other websites are not controlled by us, and we recommend reading their policies.

7. Children

The App is a general media tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you may still contact us at the address below with any request or question and we will respond.

9. Security

Your media and settings stay on your device and are protected by the security of your Mac and macOS. Please keep your device secure and up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
